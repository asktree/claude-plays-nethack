## Threats / known dangers
- **The Castle is NOT necessarily right below Medusa**: dungeon.def puts Medusa at bottom-5..bottom-2 and the Castle at the bottom. DL24 is a filler level; the Castle is DL25-27 (bottom of the Dungeons, 25-29 levels).
- **DL24's `<` leads UP onto Medusa's `>` in her hall — Medusa waits ON that staircase (asleep).** Arrive with full HP, not invisible, not blindfolded (reflection must be "seen"): her gaze reflects and stones her. Cobras/snakes, maybe a yellow light near her hall.
- Booby-trapped doors fire on opening as well as on unlocking. Nymphs/leprechauns: bag the gold.
- Fire elementals/fire breath destroy potions/scrolls (lost the speed potion). Kill them fast.
- Quantum mechanics teleport (MR doesn't stop it). Yellow lights: strike when adjacent, or wear the blindfold.
- Gelatinous cubes: never melee (passive paralysis d(7,4) turns); force bolt from 2 squares.
