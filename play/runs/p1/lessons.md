# p1 lessons (general, keep short)

- Never pick up blindly with a helper: check `here()` first (I grabbed a 350-wt locked large box). Kicking a box open shatters potions; #force with a blade risks the blade.
- Any object list must exclude shop stock (`for sale`) before choosing a corpse/loot target; a bad travel target cost 49 turns and made a fresh corpse too old.
- Corpse rule of thumb used: eat only kills < ~30 turns old, never dwarves/dogs/cats/were/cockatrice; lichen corpses never rot.
- Fight at doorway choke points: stand in the corridor square outside the door so packs (jackals, coyotes) come one at a time.
- The square just inside a shop door is the shopkeeper's home square: drops there get no offer; the shopkeeper blocks it for a turn or two — wait with `s`, then step out.
- Sell-offer price-ID: offer = base/2 (or base*3/8 for 25% of shopkeepers). Offer 10 => identify; offer 50 => 100zm ring.
- Trap doors can drop you a level before you found the downstairs: always know the `<` on arrival (search dead ends for hidden passages).
- Cropped-map column counting is error prone: use a coordinate-returning helper (`objects()`), not eyeballing.
- NEVER wield a weapon of unknown B/U/C status (picked-up orcish dagger welded itself to my hand at T:1267). If a throwaway weapon is needed, use the one already known uncursed (my +0 dagger) — or just don't.
- Prayer does NOT fix minor trouble (welded weapon, cursed items) when Luck is 0 off an altar (pray.c pleased(): action = rn1(Luck+2,1) → 1..2 fixes major only; minor needs action ≥ 3 → Luck ≥ 1). Only pray for major trouble (HP < 1/7, Weak, FoodPois, Stone, Slime, Strngl). Check the source before betting the prayer on a mechanic.
- Levels you are not on are FROZEN: a hostile left adjacent to a staircase is still there, in the same form, on every return — and it gets an attack the moment you arrive. Never re-enter a level by stairs a dangerous monster was camping; use another route (trap door, other stairs) or be ready to leave instantly.
- Werejackal @ form cannot infect; only the d-form bite does (1/4 per hit). It shifts @->d 1/5 per melee round, and d->@ only 1/30 per turn *while you are on its level*. Summons jackals 1/10 per round. With a weak weapon (regen 1/turn) it is unkillable: don't engage at all until the long sword is back.
- Lycanthropy has an upside: the transformation calls drop_weapon()/break_armor(): a WELDED cursed weapon is released/dropped and worn armor falls off (on a `>` square 1/3 of the pieces fall to the level below). Praying right after (major trouble, good odds) cures it and rehumanizes with the old HP. Used at T:2013-2016 to get rid of the welded dagger.
- 3.6 trap doors can be multi-level shafts (25%: 2+ levels). Stepping into one deliberately means arriving with no known stairs: find `<` first, keep HP full.
- Engraving with an unknown wand identifies lightning by BURNING the text: choose "Elbereth" as the text so the test also leaves a permanent safe square (but it blinds you for up to 50 turns — do it on a quiet level, standing on that square).
- Counted rests/searches are interrupted every turn while any hostile is adjacent (even a harmless acid blob): walk 2+ squares away or kill it first.
- Hungry -> Weak takes only ~100 turns (nutrition 150 -> 50 at 1/turn). Treat "Hungry" as "eat within 50 turns", not as a warning to act on later.
- Any non-blessed food item older than 30 turns (rations, candy bars...) has a 1/7 "Blecch! Rotten food!" roll; if it knocks you out, the WHOLE STACK is flagged rotten and re-rolls every time. Eat old items only on a safe square, one at a time; prefer fresh SAFE corpses (< 30 turns old) the moment Hungry appears.
- Excalibur dipping: each non-Excalibur dip rusts the sword one level (3 dips = thoroughly rusty, -3 dmg) and, once fully rusted, every failed dip has a 1/3 chance to dry the fountain. Expect to need several fountains (Oracle level has 4). The rust vanishes if Excalibur is finally made.
- Standing ON a fountain hides it from `obs.features`; use `here()`. Dust Elbereth degrades ~1/76 per turn while you stand on it and when walked over: re-engrave and read it back right before relying on it; never fight from an Elbereth square (3.6 erases it).
- Thieves (monkeys, nymphs): drop the irreplaceable items (wands, marker) on your square before they arrive, fight, pick them up after.
- Weak from hunger is a textbook major-trouble prayer (98% at ~1400 turns since the last one) — better than gambling on rotten food, but it spends the prayer for ~1000 turns: plan food so it never comes to that.
- DUST ELBERETH IS WIPED WHEN YOU STEP OFF IT: domove() calls u_wipe_engr(rnd(5)) on the square you leave, so every departure garbles 1-5 letters (read back: "Elbe?cth"). A pre-engraved refuge next to a fountain/fight square is worthless once you walk off it: engrave WHERE YOU STAND at the moment you need it (1 turn, ~72% success per try; it works before the adjacent monster's next move), and never step off and back expecting it to hold.
- Water demon from a fountain (T:3879): do not fight (AC-4, ~36 HP, 1/13 per round summons another demon). Elbereth under you makes it flee at once; a fleeing stalker does not follow you on stairs; at equal speed (12) a monster that has to move to reach you gets no melee that turn, so keep walking away and only take stairs with it 2+ squares off. Its appearance ends dipping on that level.
- Monsters know your exact position regardless of line of sight (set_apparxy: only invisibility/displacement/monster blindness fool them); breaking LOS or closing doors never sheds a pursuer. Only stairs, Elbereth, killing it, or out-running it work.
- Yellow light counter with no ranged weapon: be already Blind when it explodes (resists_blnd). NetHack 3.6 refuses throwing at yourself, but throwing a potion of blindness AT the adjacent light shatters it and (80%) you breathe the vapours: blind rnd(5) turns, "You seem unaffected by it", light dead. A blindfold/towel does the same permanently. Never melee it (~18% one-shot at my damage) and lightning/fire don't hurt it.
- Two scrolls of identify: read ONE, check the inventory, then decide — the first can identify everything (1/25 uncursed), wasting the second.
- While Blind you cannot read a DUST engraving (read_engr_at senses only engraved/burned text), so a blind dust Elbereth is unverifiable and blind engraving mis-writes more letters (1/11 extra per letter). If blindness threatens, engrave BEFORE it hits or count on a burned one; blind, rely on HP guards and F-direction attacks at the "I" marker instead.
- OBJECTS HIDE STAIRCASES: a statue, gold pile, corpse or box on a stairs square shows only the object; the stairs never appear in `obs.features`/`obs.under` and explore() will not flag it. On a "fully explored" level with a missing staircase (Sokoban `<` on Oracle+1, a `>` you can't find), step on EVERY object square in every room and read `here()` before searching walls (T:4850: the Sokoban `<` sat under a statue for 900 turns of hunting).
- Excalibur's auto-search finds secret doors as you walk past walls (a `+` appeared in a wall I had passed): re-scan the map for new doors after walking through rooms.
- Sokoban with sokoban.solve(): when a pause (monster, Hungry) interrupts a multi-push step, progress() reports done=-1 and solve() refuses; the board is just mid-step. Compare with `sokoban._diff(sokoban._state(obs, lv, ox, oy), after_step_k)` and finish that boulder's remaining moves with `sokoban.push(x, y, keys)` — then solve() resumes. Happened 3 times in 3 levels.
- A monster (even asleep) on a hole square blocks the boulder meant for that hole: kill it first — from the previous, already plugged square; never step onto an unplugged hole.
- On no-teleport levels (Sokoban) a nymph can't escape with the loot: attack her from the mouth of a dead end, keep hitting, take the items back from her corpse. Elsewhere drop wands/marker first.
- Peaceful monsters in 1-wide Sokoban corridors: you cannot swap or push past them in 3.6; wait in sight of the corridor and slip past diagonally when they reach a junction (~90 turns for a gnome lord). Use the wait productively (engrave-test wands, eat).
- Sokoban level items (food rations, rings, wands, a bag) appear when boulders move or holes are plugged: sweep the level with `here()` before leaving; never eat the eggs.
- 3.6.7 Elbereth: ANY attack from the square — melee, throwing, AND ZAPPING a wand — triggers setmangry(): "You feel like a hypocrite", -5 alignment, engraving deleted. Rest on Elbereth; step off it (one square) before fighting or zapping.
- Scared adjacent monsters do not interrupt counted rests on an Elbereth square (monster_nearby() skips them), so `rest_on_elbereth` works with a hovering ogre; but a monster that keeps stepping in and out of view costs a harness pause each time — rest where only the approach square is visible (beside a doorway, not in line with the room).
- Big-monster diagonal squeeze rule is NOT reliable: a python (MZ_LARGE) came through a boulder+wall diagonal that a huge ettin zombie could not. Plan chokepoints on walls/doors, not on boulder diagonals.
- Fresh corpses of resistant monsters are cheap intrinsic rolls: python (poison 40%), gelatinous cube (fire/cold/sleep/shock; acidic: 1d15), red naga (fire/poison 20% each -> gave POISON RESISTANCE). Check `corpse()`, eat only with nothing in view; long corpses (naga 2600 wt = ~40 turns) get interrupted but can be resumed.
- Lightning ray geometry: range is 7-13 squares total; it is safe to zap toward a wall only if (distance to wall)*2 > 13, i.e. the first wall in line is 7+ squares away; otherwise the bounce comes back onto you (and destroys your own wands/rings). A bolt through a lined-up wand-carrier destroys its wand ("breaks apart and explodes").
- Fire gazes/breath (pyrolisk, red naga) still burn armor and roll scroll/potion destruction even when you are fire resistant: kill them fast or break line of sight; a ring of fire resistance at least keeps the HP.
- Sokoban 4a: a giant mimic posed inside the boulder field next to the wall gap (34,10) and hit for 24 in one round; any "boulder" the solver doesn't list, or a monster appearing where a boulder was, is a mimic — farlook before walking past.
- Wand of striking (force bolt, no bounce) is the right tool for a gelatinous cube: 2 zaps at range, no passive paralysis risk.
- NEVER engrave while hallucinating: 3.6.7 engrave.c replaces each letter with 50% probability under Hallu (1/7 confused, 1/4 stunned, 1/11 blind): two Elbereths came out as "$8bVqvtG" / "/l!vUeUb". Wait it out first (a black light's blast lasts 10d12 turns).
- Black lights are permanently invisible (like stalkers): an unexplained "It explodes! ... kaleidoscopic light" or a "Wait! There's something there you can't see!" in a quiet corridor can be one. An invisible monster steps aside instead of attacking 1/3 of the time, so "You attack thin air" after a hit does not mean it fled far.
- Thrown missiles fly OVER boulders (melee does not reach): use them on a monster parked behind a Sokoban boulder — but restricted-skill darts hit ~35% and cursed ones "slip" 1/7. Cheaper: a failed push costs no game time, so alternate `s` and the push; a stuck monster shuffles among the squares nearest you and the square frees within ~10 cycles.
- A KICKED-OPEN door is BROKEN and broken doorways allow diagonal movement: the "one attacker" chokepoint outside a zoo door only holds for an intact open door. With a broken door the square diagonal to the doorway (50,16) also gets attacked (a monkey slipped through it and stole a worn ring). Prefer #force/unlocking, or stand so the doorway's diagonals are walls.
- Yellow light + already Blind = harmless ("You seem unaffected by it."): when a yellow light is queued in a zoo, being blinded by something mild (dust vortex) first is fine; do not move while blind if a cockatrice corpse lies nearby (feeling it bare-handed is instant stoning).
- Cockatrice in melee with a wielded weapon is safe from the passive; fight() needs allow_passive=True; each of its touches has ~1/30 to start stoning — prayer is the cure when no lizard/acidic corpse is carried.
- Scripting: never match item text with a bare substring like 'cursed ...' — "uncursed" contains "cursed" (sold the wrong NR 9 scroll). Use a regex with a word boundary (r'\bcursed\b') or check `buc` fields. Sell prompts say "Sell it?" for singles and "Sell them?" for stacks: match r'Sell (it|them)\?'.
- Soldier ants (speed 18, 2 attacks, ~14/round each) arrive in threes; three adjacent = ~25 HP/turn even at AC3. They are NOT stalkers: they never follow you up or down stairs. Fight them ON a staircase square one blow at a time and climb the moment HP nears 55%; never try to out-rest them on dust Elbereth.
- 3.6.x dust Elbereth: each monster scared by it erodes one letter (100% for dust). Against one hovering monster it lasts; against 2-3 fast monsters it breaks within a few turns and re-engraving costs a full round of hits each time. It is a one-monster tool, or a burned one.
- Level arrival ambushes: monsters frozen next to a staircase attack the moment you arrive (soldier ants at the Minetown `<`, the water demon at the DL6 `>`). Arrive at full HP, and before pressing `<`/`>` know which square you will be standing on and what your escape is (the stairs themselves if the attackers don't stalk).
- Shop price-ID with the harness: `price_id('TOOL_CLASS', buy=75)` uses the real 3.6.7 tables — the "magic lamp" base cost is 50 (not 500 as I misremembered), so a "lamp" that costs 75 at Ch7 can only be a MAGIC LAMP. Always run price_id() instead of recalling base prices.
- Selling to a general store price-IDs everything for free (offer = base/2 at normal shks): unknown scrolls/rings/wands you don't want become both gold and knowledge; cursed junk sells at full price. A cross-aligned temple priest still sells protection (400 x XL; first purchase 2-4 AC, got 3).
- Under hallucination in town, an adjacent monster that "misses"/"hits" you is hostile by definition (peacefuls never attack); when it is the ONLY adjacent monster, one forced F-blow is safe (it can't be a floating eye: those have no active attack).
