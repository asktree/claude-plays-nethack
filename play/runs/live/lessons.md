# live — lessons

- Pet-test EVERY unidentified armor piece before wearing, shop-bought included: on DL2, 2 of 5 bought pieces
  (high boots, buckled boots) were cursed. Drop in a corridor, step away, wait for "steps reluctantly over".
- Little dogs pick up and carry dropped items (a helm, a food ration): watch for "drops" and fetch it.
- When explore() says "explored" with no `>`: reason about which walls face big unexplored areas (room join
  logic) and search there; the DL1 stairs room was behind a hidden door on the west room's west wall.
- Locked large boxes: #force with the DAGGER (swap with `x`), never the long sword (future Excalibur; blades can break).
- Hunger comes fast (~900 turns per ration): the first prayer for Weak (T:2717) was the right call to save food. Small
  corpses (giant ant 10, gnome 100) barely help; pick up every food ration seen (farlook `%` items).
- A pet picking up an item proves it isn't cursed (dog carried the iron shoes): faster than waiting for "reluctantly".
- An unknown "bag" can be a bag of tricks: apply it only at full HP with an escape; it's a decent XP source.
- A hostile MUMAK (difficulty 7) does 4d12+2d6: it took 28 HP in one round twice at AC-3. In a DARK town it appears
  adjacent without warning — travel/explore don't stop in time. Kill-or-avoid it before exploring a dark level it roams;
  Elbereth works on it (and on ghosts). Fighting another monster with it nearby was the mistake (T:3177).
- Bones levels: a grave with a headstone "killed by ..." = the dead player's full kit. Everything is 80% cursed: altar-test
  before using. An artifact in bones (Excalibur) blocks making your own (exist_artifact).
- Engrave-testing a wand of digging leaves an ENGRAVED Elbereth — a durable safe square.
- Price-ID in a general store with sell_offer(): a dozen items in ~20 turns; learn the shk's rate first.
