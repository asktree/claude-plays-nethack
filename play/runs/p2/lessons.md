# p2 lessons

- Before ANY directional action (kick, throw, zap, F-fight), re-check the target square's occupant in the same step: pets wander onto boxes/items between turns. Never let a script run a directional action in a loop with only a "hostiles nearby" check.
- Never call helpers that send keys (`inventory()`, `here()`) unless `obs.kind == 'command'`; they can type into an open prompt.
- Shops: `m`-steps give no "You see here" line; read `obs.messages` right after `do()` (before `look()`); sell-offer price ID works (drop, decline with `n`, `,` to take back — mind piles that open a menu).
- NetHack travel (`_`) routes through closed doors and pushable boulders and then stops; use waypoints, or clear the obstacle (kick door open, push boulder to a dead end) on levels you will cross often.
- Sell-offer price ID (drop / decline / pick up) DESTROYS a scroll of scare monster that was already picked up once ("turns to dust"). Never drop-probe an unknown scroll that was picked up from the floor unless its group can't be scare monster (base 100 group can). Lost one on D4 T:1662.
- `travel()` never autopicks up (NetHack sets nopick for the travel command): after travelling to gold or a thrown dagger, press `,` or take the last step with a plain move.
- Inside a tended shop you can never swap places with your pet ("You stop. Your kitten is in the way!"); wait a turn instead of retrying. Outside shops a swap fails 1 in 7.
- Item piles in rooms can hide undiscovered traps (D3 "food pile" = sleeping gas trap; slept ~20 turns). Prefer walking to piles only when the level is quiet; consider searching/`^` when a lone item sits in an odd spot.
- Locked chests: kick from an adjacent square ("THUD!" = no luck, 1 in 5 breaks the lock); re-check the chest square for the pet before every kick. `#loot` → `o` → "All types" → item menu (filter out corpses: no gloves = never touch a cockatrice corpse).
- Yellow molds: two thrown daggers kill most of them; one sword blow to finish is acceptable (passive = short stun only if it survives). The kitten steals kills — fight things myself when XP matters, keep the kitten behind me in corridors.
- Werejackals (D3–D4 at XL3): throw a dagger while they approach, then melee; check `history` afterwards for "You feel feverish" (fight() folds earlier rounds' messages). Prayer (major trouble) is the cure; verify `prayer_check()` first.
- Throwing: check the whole LINE OF FIRE for the pet, not just the target square (T:2100 the orcish dagger hit the housecat standing right next to me in the corridor). If the pet is in line, wait a turn or melee instead.
- Leprechauns: drop all gold (`d`,`$`) a few squares away, then melee — with no gold on you or under you its hit steals nothing and it does not teleport; killed one in 2 blows for ~35 Exp (XL4->5). A leprechaun in a pit is a free kill. Its corpse pile held 75 gold.
- Dead-end corridors on a level whose `>` is missing: search there first (D4 (9,15): 2 turns). Diagonal steps between two rock squares need inventory weight <= 600 ("carrying too much to get through") — use another entrance.
- Fountain dipping rusts a non-blessed sword every dip and can curse it (1/30, silent: check the inventory line) — that is only minor trouble; Excalibur (1/6 per dip at XL5) uncurses, de-rusts and rustproofs it. Got it on dip 2 at the Oracle's fountains.
- Never send a raw movement key toward a target in a loop: in the Mines a peaceful can step into the square first ("Really attack the gnome?"). Use `safe_step()` (checks the destination for any monster) or `travel()`.
- A pet that is eating does not follow you downstairs ("The housecat is still eating."): after it kills something near the stairs, wait ~3 + corpse_weight/64 turns (gnome: ~13) before using them.
- Before picking up a `*`, read farlook's parenthetical: "(a rock)" / "(some stones)" / "(a gray stone)" = leave it (rocks weigh 10 each; gray stones may be loadstones). Only "(a <colour> gem)" is worth taking.
- Minetown does not always look like a cave from the stairs: this one has solid-rock outskirts with `#` corridors and two small walled stair rooms; the town (shops, fountain, watchmen) is in the middle. Temple sounds ("carcass offered in sacrifice", "praising <god>") identify the level and the altar's alignment (god of your pantheon: Tyr lawful / Odin neutral / Loki chaotic).
- A "lamp" when "oil lamp" is already identified is a MAGIC LAMP: keep it, get it blessed (holy water) before #rubbing for the wish.
- Cold-resistant Valkyries can melee brown molds safely; still never melee yellow/green molds or acid blobs with a non-erodeproof weapon. Excalibur is rustproof (erodeproof: rust AND corrosion).
- Altar BUC test: `D` → `X` (unknown B/U/C) → select items (2 pages: `>` for page 2) → `<CR>`; flashes: amber = blessed, black = cursed, "lands on the altar" = uncursed; `,` + select all takes everything back with the same letters. Exclude scrolls that were already picked up once and could be scare monster (base-100 group) — they'd turn to dust on re-pickup.
- Enchant weapon: blessed on a +2 weapon gives +1..+3 ("glows blue for a while" = 2–3), uncursed +1; the evaporation check only applies when the weapon is already above +5, so +2 → +6 with two scrolls is safe. "Vibrates unexpectedly" = it is now above +5: stop enchanting.
- Shops: the square just inside the door is the shopkeeper's post — no sell offer there; stand one square further in. Shopkeeper names give the shop type before entering (Kilmihil = books, Pasawahan = deli, AlliWar Wickson = hardware, Izchak = lights). Buying an unidentified "whistle" is a 23% magic-whistle gamble (tin whistle otherwise).
- A shop container can hold gold: picking it up creates a "You owe X N zorkmids" loan for the gold, which you then own — take it out of the bag after paying.
- Disguised mimics (`]`/objects) never move or attack (mon.c movemon skips hiders with M_AP_OBJECT/M_AP_FURNITURE); walking next to them is safe as long as you never step onto them or search (`s`) adjacent. Wait with `.` instead of `s` near one.
- One identify scroll can identify several items (uncursed: 1 in 5 chance of 2+); order the "identify next?" menu picks by value: unknown amulet > unknown bag > unknown wand (unless engrave-testable) > scrolls > potions.
- Engrave-test every unknown wand before spending identify on it; combine with the shop base price ("bugs stop moving" + base 175 = sleep, not death).
- In 3.6 shopkeepers quote fake prices for unidentified gems (otyp-based), so gem sell offers do not identify them.
- `go_down()` picks the NEAREST `>` — when standing on a branch staircase (D2 Mines stairs) that is the one under you. On levels with two `>`, use `travel(x, y)` + verify `obs.under == '>'` + `>` explicitly (T:3947).
- LEPRECHAUN HALL technique (T:4171–4238, 18 kills, 0 damage, ~2700 gold): put ALL gold into a sack first (`bag_put('n','$')`; leprechauns can't steal from containers, and with no gold to take a woken one just claws 1d2 and does not teleport); with Stealth the sleepers never wake on their own — kill them one at a time with F-attacks from gold-free squares (squares next to the room's first door hold no gold); walk a cleared column to autopickup its gold, bag it, then attack the next column from there. An awake leprechaun hops away 1 time in 7 ("You miss wildly and stumble forwards"). Never eat leprechaun corpses (teleportitis). ~34 Exp each at XL6-7.
- Gas spores are mindless: telepathy does not show them, and in a dark corridor an unseen one 2 squares away is invisible. `last_seen()` + a blind throw down the corridor from 2+ squares kills it safely ("You kill it!" with no blast damage).
- The 3.6 identify scroll can identify 2+ items: order picks by value (unknown ring > base-300 potion > unknown scroll group > new potions).
- YELLOW LIGHTS: `fight_until_clear()`/`fight()` will happily melee one that drifts adjacent (it explodes: Blind ~80 turns). When a `y` yellow light is within 3 squares, stop the loop and handle it by hand (thrown dagger, wand, or walk away); never let an auto-fight loop run with one near (T:5573).
- BLINDED with the amulet of ESP on: telepathy shows every monster with a mind on the level, so I saw the killer-bee hive from the stairs. Blind in a crowd = go upstairs immediately (only adjacent monsters follow) and wait it out on the quiet level.
- KILLER BEE HIVES in the open (Big Room, D10 T:5668–5765): without poison resistance each landed sting is a 1/240 instadeath and a hive is 7+ bees at speed 18 — a stair-dance only splits them, it does not remove the exposure. Bypass: dig down elsewhere (a hole lands you at a RANDOM spot, not the same x,y!), or fight only where a single bee can reach you. Bag scrolls/potions/spellbooks before a pyrolisk (fire gaze burns them at range; containers protect).
- DIGGING: applying the pick-axe WIELDS it (re-wield Excalibur at once); digging down stops at the pit stage and needs a second apply; a dwarf's effort doubles each turn (pit in 2 turns, hole in ~2 more). ELBERETH CAN BE ENGRAVED FROM INSIDE YOUR OWN PIT and it works: bee, centaur and dust vortex all fled while I finished the hole (T:5814–5817).
- A mountain centaur that "picks up a potion" will throw it: white potion = paralysis vapors ("Something seems to be holding you") — kill or avoid potion-carrying throwers early.
- A lawful altar + a prayer with prayer timeout 0 = holy water for every potion of water lying on the altar. Stockpile water first (dilute junk potions twice in a fountain), then ONE prayer blesses them all; don't waste it on a single potion.
- Getobj prompts re-ask after "You don't have that object." — a scripted `d`+letter loop must Esc out, or the next command key becomes an item letter (I dropped the magic lamp by accident, T:5822).
- MAGIC LAMP WISH IS NOT GUARANTEED: a blessed lamp gives the djinni's wish 80% of the time; I drew the 5% "Thank you for freeing me!" tame djinni (T:6114) after a whole holy-water operation. Never make magic resistance depend on a single lamp; keep a plan B (cloak of MR from shops/monsters, gray dragon scales, Castle wand) and treat the pet djinni as the consolation prize.
- HOLY WATER RECIPE (worked T:6084–6113): dilute a junk potion twice in a fountain (`dip()`; "dilutes" then "dilutes further" -> clear potion = uncursed water; ~1/6 per dip the fountain dries, 1/60 water demon/nymph/snakes), drop every water ON a co-aligned altar, `prayer_check()` (no trouble -> timeout must be 0), `pray(force=True)`: "The potions on the altar glow light blue" = holy water. Then `#dip` the item into the water: cursed -> "glows amber" (uncursed) -> "glows with a light blue aura" (blessed). Answer the "Call a clear potion:" prompt with "water".
- GREMLINS AT NIGHT (real clock 22:00–06:00 in the game's timezone): a landed curse claw can remove an intrinsic (speed/stealth/cold res). Sleep-ray them from range or fight only by day. Sleep wand + Excalibur on the sleeper = zero-risk kill.
- travel()'s LAST STEP is a plain move: if a monster steps onto the target square first, you attack it (T:5952 cockatrice). With a weapon wielded that is fine; never travel onto a square next to a cockatrice while bare-handed, and re-check the target square before the last step in scripts.
- Container menus show IDENTIFIED names ("potion of paralysis"), not appearances ("white potion"): pattern bag_take() by the identified name when the type is known.
- A monster that quaffs a potion identifies it: "looks better" = healing, "looks much better" = extra healing, "looks completely healed" = full healing (effervescent, T:5915).
- THROWING INSIDE A SHOP SELLS THE PROJECTILE (3.6 dothrow check_shop_obj -> sellobj, no prompt): "You relinquish a dagger and receive N gold pieces in compensation." Things thrown INTO a shop from outside become shop stock too. To kill a floating eye in a shop, accept buying the daggers back (base-4 daggers: sold 2–7, rebought 5–19), or lure/avoid.
- Items dropped by a monster that dies inside a shop are shop stock ("for sale") — the large dog's twisted ring cost 267 zm and was a CURSED ring of teleportation: price-ID first (base 150/200 group here), never put on unknown rings.
- A floating eye corpse in a shop costs ~7 zm ("You bite that, you pay for it!") — cheapest intrinsic telepathy there is; `pay()` afterwards.
- SPHERES (shocking/flaming/freezing) and gas spores are MINDLESS: neither telepathy kind shows them. In dark corridors assume one can appear adjacent and explode (shocking: 4d6, may destroy wands/rings).
- A monster that MOVES next to you cannot attack in the same action (monmove.c: after m_move it only attacks if it did not move). Against slow dangerous monsters (chickatrice speed 4) wait for them to step adjacent, then strike: you get ~4 actions before their next one. Throwing daggers down the line first softens them.
- Inside a pit you only see adjacent squares and monsters lose track of you; climbing out takes turns. Unknown traps hide in dark rooms: autosearch does not find them in time when travelling.
- Unlocking a box with a key in 3.6: apply key, answer the direction prompt with `.` (self), then "unlock it? y".
- Booby-trapped doors explode when opened (damage + stun): wait out Stun with `search` before moving.
- A co-aligned altar: drop ALL unknown-BUC items with `D` → `X` → `.` (select all pages) → `<CR>`, then `,` → `.` → `<CR>`. Keep the pack under 52 items or bag_take() silently stops ("Your knapsack cannot accommodate any more items").
- Energy vortex engulf: harmless for a non-caster (1d6 + Pw drain); 2–3 blows from inside with F+dir.
- SACRIFICE = PRAYER-TIMEOUT PROBE (pray.c dosacrifice): with the timeout > 0 a fresh sacrifice says "You have a hopeful feeling" (reduced, still > 0) or "You have a feeling of reconciliation" (now 0); once it is 0 the sacrifice gives Luck ("You glimpse a four-leaf clover" / blind: "You think something brushed your foot") and a 1/10 gift roll. Two Luck messages at T:7124/7128 proved the timeout was 0 although prayer_check() estimated only ~88%.
- CREATE-MONSTER ALTAR CAMPING: good XP (+835 Exp in ~10 zaps) but few usable corpses: most monsters leave one only 1 time in 3 (big ones always), elementals/golems never, and many corpses are too heavy to carry onto the altar (humanoids ~1000–1450: quantum mechanic, Uruk-hai). Light fresh corpses (raven 40, imp 20, iguana 30) are the offerable ones. 1 zap in 23 makes 2–8 monsters (I got 7 at once): zap only at >= 95% HP, never with a prompt/plan half done.
- FIRE ELEMENTAL melee: its passive fire (d(lvl+1,4), 2/3 of the hits it survives) plus its claw cost 27 HP in one exchange; it leaves no corpse. Kill it in as few blows as possible or avoid.
- POISONOUS CORPSE for poison resistance: 80% "Ecch - that must have been poisonous!" = -1d4 Str (I lost 3: 17 → 14) and it lowers carrying capacity by 25 per point — drop junk BEFORE eating when near the weight limit. "You feel healthy." = poison resistance gained (scorpion 50%).
- Raven blinding stacks: each landed claw adds 1d6 turns; one fight gave ~30 turns blind. With telepathy you still see every minded monster and can keep fighting.
- YELLOW LIGHT: it explodes only as its own attack when it starts its turn next to you; kill it with one melee blow after softening it with thrown daggers (4 dagger hits did not kill it; Excalibur did).
- QUANTUM MECHANIC: its claw teleports you on the level (you lose position, it comes back); never eat its corpse with intrinsic speed (toggles it off).
- Step directions: to reach a square, use travel(x, y) instead of a hand-picked direction key (I sent 'y' instead of 'h' and rested off the altar).
